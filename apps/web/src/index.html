Arbitron

Finds freelance marketplace jobs that match your saved searches, scores each one, prices what delivering it would cost, checks the margin against your own rules, and drafts a bid for you to approve.

How it works

  1. Find

    Your saved searches pull in new jobs from the marketplaces you connect.

  2. Qualify

    Each job is scored for fit, client quality, competition and red flags, with the reasons.

  3. Price

    Delivery is estimated from your own rates, your suppliers or market bands, and the margin is worked out after platform fees, supplier cost and your FX buffer.

  4. Contact

    Only jobs that clear your margin rule get a draft bid, written from your own templates.

  5. Brief

    When a client replies, a discovery conversation turns their answers into a structured brief.

  6. Source

    Decide who delivers: your own team, an AI-assisted build, or a supplier, ranked from your database or found through a sourcing post.

  7. Deliver

    Won work is tracked through milestones, supplier handover, delivery and payment.

  8. Learn

    Reply rate, win rate and realised margin per category, template, supplier and search.

Safeguards

  • Every bid, message and sourcing post waits for your approval, on the web or in Telegram. The only exceptions are ones you switch on yourself: auto-send for a saved search, which starts off and has a daily cap, and a first reply you write for when you are away.
  • Live mode starts switched off, and cannot be switched on until your margin rules, fee table and data-retention period are set. While it is off, nothing is sent: what would have been sent is logged instead.
  • No fee or price is guessed. Margins use the fee table you enter from each platform's own fee page, with its address and date.
  • Marketplaces are reached through their official APIs only. Arbitron never drives a browser signed in to a marketplace.
  • Your organisation's data is kept apart from every other organisation's.
  • Every state change and outside call is written to an audit log you can read.
