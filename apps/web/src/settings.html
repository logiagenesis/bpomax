Settings

Live mode

Two switches must both be on before any bid, message or post reaches a marketplace. The first is on the server; the second is this organisation's.

Server switch: Off

Sandbox

Live mode cannot be switched on until these are set:

    Margin rules

    Every rule is required before live mode. Nothing here has a default: a blank rule means no job is judged until it is set (docs/02 D-02, D-03, T-06).

    Held back on deals not in rand.

    Rand prices are shown VAT-inclusive at this rate where VAT applies.

    How long a closed conversation keeps client content. From legal advice (T-06).

    Fee table

    One rule per platform, project type and side, read from the platform's official fee page. The page and the day it was read are required (docs/02 T-02).

    Platform accounts

    One account per verified identity. The plan and its monthly bid allowance are read from the platform's membership page (docs/02 T-03); the day recorded is kept.

    Connecting needs the Freelancer.com developer app and sandbox accounts (docs/02 B-03 and B-04; ARB-020).

    Scanners

    A scanner is a saved search that runs on a timer. Auto-send is off unless switched on here, and then only with a daily cap and a minimum score.
