Templates

The templates bids are drafted from, and each one's variants side by side. New bids are written from a template's switched-on variants in turn, so each gets an even share. A variant's reply rate is the bids sent from it that had a client message after them, of the bids sent from it, counted from the stored bids and messages. Once a variant has been sent, its words stay as they are, so its rate keeps measuring them: to try new words, add a variant, and switch off the one that does worse.

New template
