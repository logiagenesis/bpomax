Analytics

How the bids have done, grouped by category, template, supplier or scanner. Every figure is counted from stored rows over the jobs with a bid sent. Reply rate is the bids that had a client message after them, of the bids; win rate is the jobs won, of those won or lost; realised margin is payments in less payments out, in rand; cost per reply is the model spend on those jobs, of the replies. A rate with nothing under it says so.
